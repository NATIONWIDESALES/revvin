- Moved public pages are PrerenderRoute entries with redirectTo (meta refresh + canonical document) plus a client <Navigate replace>. Why: hosting has no server-side 301s.
- sitemap.xml and llms-full.txt are generated at build by plugins/sitemap.ts and plugins/prerender.ts from PRERENDER_ROUTES; never hand-write public/sitemap.xml. Why: the sitemap cannot drift from the built, indexable pages.
- Brand colours are HSL tokens in src/index.css (forest, green, sign, paper, line) mapped to shadcn vars; marketing layout classes use the mk-/rf-/yard- prefixes there. Why: one source for the yard-sign design across pages.
- Business dashboard navigation is the DashboardShell rail; tab ids and next-step/stat logic live in src/lib/dashboardHome.ts. Why: deep links (?tab=) and Home numbers stay testable in one place.
- Public routes are server-rendered at build (src/entry-server.tsx via plugins/ssr.ts) into each prerendered document with data-ssr="1"; src/main.tsx hydrates those and client-renders everything else. Referral pages get their business row through window.__REVVIN_SSR__ (src/lib/ssrData.ts). Why: crawlers and first paint get the real page, and hydration needs identical first-render data.
- Fixed public page titles/descriptions live in src/content/pageMeta.ts and are read by both seoRoutes.ts and each page's SEOHead; src/test/seo-pass.test.ts enforces lengths. Why: prerendered head and hydrated head cannot disagree.
- The backend client is loaded with loadSupabase() from src/lib/supabaseLazy.ts, never statically imported by marketing code; tslib is pinned to the react chunk in vite.config.ts. Why: keeps the backend bundle off the initial download of marketing pages.
- Fonts are self-hosted from src/assets/fonts with metric-matched fallbacks in src/index.css; the prerender preloads the two hero weights and inlines critical CSS (beasties) on server-rendered documents. Why: no third-party render-blocking stylesheet.
